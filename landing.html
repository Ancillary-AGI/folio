Questions an engineer
actually asks.

Is the simulator a real SPICE, or a visual approximation?

A real SPICE. MNA with Newton–Raphson companion stamps, Gummel–Poon BJTs with Early voltages and junction capacitances, and level-1 Shichman–Hodges MOSFETs with body effect, channel-length modulation and gate capacitances. JFET level-1, op-amp companions and vendor model cards are all parsed and evaluated; the vendor library ships the actual IRF540N, 2N7000 and 2N3055 parameters.

Is the router really routing, or is it coloured lines?

It routes. A rip-up-and-retry A* grid router runs on the real board geometry against the nets your schematic declared. Vias go inside declared via-span rules, skew is measured for the DDR and byte-lane groups it handles, and what it cannot route is reported by net and layer — not drawn anyway.

What won't the agent do?

Invent component values, claim the simulation passed when it has not, add nets you did not agree to, or run signal integrity or thermal analysis — neither exists in Folio, and neither will be faked. When it cannot do the job, it says so and stops.

Is the local build really free, or will it start charging?

Free indefinitely. The standalone build has no account, no upload, no telemetry — it cannot accept payment and it will not start tomorrow. Pro and Team add the cloud doors only: hosted sync, the publish button, the admin console. Local stays unlimited and stays free.

Where is my design right now?

On your disk. Projects, checkpoints, VCS branches, exports and simulations all live in this browser's local storage on this machine. Exports are plain files — Gerber, Excellon, CSV, STEP — never a proprietary blob a vendor can hold hostage.

What does Folio ship to a fab house?

Gerber RS-274X per film, Excellon with blind/buried span support, centroid CSV for SMT assembly, BOM with lifecycle status, and a DRC printout. The Gerbers are CRC-verified. If a fab house won't accept the files, you will find out before you spend money — from the DRC list.

What does it cost today?

Nothing. Pro at $19 and Team at $49 per user per month are announced pricing for when billing exists — not a charge that starts today. Enterprise is a signed conversation: private deployment, SSO, dedicated seats, SLA.

How is Folio tested?

The gate is npm run verify and it fails the build. It covers type-check, strict lint with zero allowed warnings, accessibility checks (icon-button names against 460+ files), and thousands of assertions across 338 test files — solver accuracy pinned against closed-form references, router steered through cases known not to fit, DRC violations firing at the exact geometry that causes them, CRC32 on every byte of a fab package.
