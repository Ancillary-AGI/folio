The pipelineThree moves
to a manufacturable board.

Open the workbench

Describe it

“Two-cell buck, 3.3 V at 2 A, USB-C in, differential pair on the data lines.” The assistant drafts the parts, the netlist and the placement — every choice visible and editable.

Review it

The workbench runs ERC, the solver, and the DRC after every edit. The agent proposes; you approve. Nothing reaches the fab gate without the checks clearing.

Ship it

One click: Gerber RS-274X per film, Excellon with blind/buried spans, centroid, BOM — one verified folio-fab-package.zip. The checks never lie about being checks.

The tourYour first board,
six moves.

Start free
01 · Describe

Say it in plain words

“Two-cell buck, 3.3 V at 2 A, USB-C in, differential pair on the data lines.”

  • The assistant drafts parts, nets and placement
  • Every choice is visible, editable and undoable
  • No black box — the plan is readable before you accept it
02 · Synthesize

The sheet builds itself

The schematic lands on the grid: symbols, values, references, net labels — all tied to the real catalogue, all yours to edit.

  • ERC fires after every edit, not at the end
  • Every part is placed, unplaced is an error
  • Undo any single move, not just whole demos
03 · Check

Checks after every keystroke

ERC finds unconnected pins, driver conflicts, power-to-ground shorts. Simulation runs DC, transient, AC, noise and Monte Carlo.

  • Every finding names the pin, the net and the rule
  • F8 steps through findings; click to locate
  • Sim traces are computed, not drawn
04 · Route

Copper you can inspect

Grid-placed router on the real board; every net either routes or is named. No copper invented, ever.

  • 52 / 66 nets on the mainboard demo, measured
  • Poured planes on inner films, with anti-pads
  • DRC gates the ship decision — not a checkbox
05 · See it

The board in 3D, not a drawing

Orbit the exact board you routed — parts, copper, silk, connectors. Export STEP for enclosure design.

  • Same geometry the fab sees
  • STEP export, mechanical fit check
  • Split viewport: schematic ∥ 3D, or floating PiP
06 · Ship

One zip. Every film.

Gerber RS-274X, Excellon, centroid, BOM — DRC-gated, CRC-verified, one archive.

  • Works offline — no service, no upload, no telemetry
  • BOM with cost & lifecycle status
  • Publish as a CC-BY template for others to fork

PrinciplesHonest by construction.

Computed, not mocked

Every number is worked

Solvers, routers and checkers are real code with published methods and machine-checked test counts. No screenshots of analysis; no “AI confidence”.

Limits, stated

Refusals, not guesses

When Folio won’t model something, it says so — in the app, in the docs, in the register. A blank is better than a fabricated answer.

Yours to build on

Open by default

Demos are CC-BY seeds you can fork. App is yours offline; publish when you’re proud of it, and get forked like a repo.
