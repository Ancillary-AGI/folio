Straight answers.

Is Folio actually free?

Yes. The standalone build runs the whole stack on your machine — schematic, solver, router, DRC, fab package, simulations — with no account, no subscription and no telemetry. Pro and Team add the cloud doors: hosted storage, publishing and the admin console, at announced prices.

Does it work offline?

Completely. Projects, checkpoints, exports, simulations and community templates all live on your machine. Publishing to the community needs a deployment — that's a cloud service, and it's honest about being one.

What is the agent actually doing?

It calls the same tools you call — placing parts, wiring pins, running the solver, reading ERC findings — then reports what it did. Every step is visible, reversible, and names its assumptions. Nothing it does is hidden from you, and nothing it can't do is claimed either.

How is this different from a photo of a board?

Every demo and template is a real project: real solver, real router, real DRC gate, real fab package. The tests are the contract. Count them.
