Questions an engineer asks first

Is Local really free and unlimited?
Yes. The standalone build runs on your machine with no account, no upload, no telemetry. Your designs never leave your disk unless you publish or export them.
When does billing start?
It has not shipped. Pro and Team will charge only when it does — never retroactively — and Local stays free and unlimited.
What counts as a user?
A person with their own identity. On Team, an administrator assigns engineer, viewer or owner roles per person; quotas are enforced by the database, not by the UI.
Can I buy Pro today?
No. There is no checkout yet, and this page does not fake one. Leave an address via the Pro link and you will be told when it opens.
Do I keep my designs if I cancel Pro?
Always. Exports — STEP, Gerber, BOM, JSON, fab package — are free on every tier, and everything you made stays on your machine where Local still works.
What about community designs?
CC-BY and CC0 licensed, free to open and adopt. Attribution travels with every copy. In Local you share them as files; in Pro you can publish them for the whole community.
What does the admin console do?
It manages the deployment: members, roles, quota policies, usage ledger and the audit trail. It is not the CAD, and a designer never sees it.
Can we run Folio in our VPC?
That is Enterprise: private deployment, SSO, data residency, retention controls, and a named engineer. Email founder@folio.dev.
