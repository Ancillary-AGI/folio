The guided tour

Six steps,
one verified board.

The same path a hardware engineer takes with Folio — from an empty brief to a fab-ready package with the DRC gate cleared. Every step, in order, with nothing hidden.

Start designing Browse community designs
01

Describe it

One brief — power rail, key parts, interface constraints, fab house. The assistant drafts the topology from the same tools a human would use, and shows its work: the netlist, the BOM, the pinout, every net named.

  • The plan reads as a spec before you accept it
  • Every choice — part number, voltage rail, impedance — editable
  • No invented parts; the catalogue is the ground truth
02

Synthesize it

The schematic lands on the grid: symbols, values, references, net labels, power and ground rails. ERC fires after every edit; nothing reaches the sim until the sheet is clean.

  • Every unplaced part is an error, not a warning
  • Undo any single move — nothing is one monolithic operation
  • The sheet exports cleanly for kiraw write discipline
03

Verify it

DC, transient, AC, noise and Monte Carlo. The exact same compiled solver runs in the browser and in the fab gate. Every finding names the pin, the net, and the rule it breaks.

  • F8 steps findings; click anywhere on the sheet to jump
  • Traces are computed and plotted — never drawn as decoration
  • Vendor model cards are parsed, not guessed
04

Route it

A rip-up-and-retry A* grid router over the real board. Every net either routes or it is reported by net and by layer. No copper drawn because the renderer likes the symmetry.

  • Via-span rules enforced per layer stack
  • Differential-pair skew tracked for the ports that matter
  • DRC rejects and names what fails before the fab gate opens
05

See it

The routed board, in 3D, with the real 3D footprints. STEP export for the enclosure designer. Split viewport so schematic and 3D share the same canvas.

  • Parts, copper, silk, mounting holes — the same geometry a fab house sees
  • STEP and faceted STEP export for enclosure design
  • STEP export is verified against the footprint catalogue
06

Ship it

Gerber RS-274X, Excellon with blind/buried spans, centroid CSV, BOM, CRC-verified archive. One file. One zip. Every film, every drill, every hole — and the DRC report to hand it to a fab with.

  • The verification you'd have done by hand is applied by the gate
  • CRC32 over every byte; a fab rejection is not a risk
  • CC-BY template for the community once you're ready

See it, then try it.

Pick a template from the community and break it — that's what CC-BY is for.
